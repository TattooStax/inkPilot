# Tattoo Stencil Production

Start with latest approved master and recorded revision/dimensions. Prefer shared vector paths for shaded and outline exports. Attach actual source references to editing tools; filenames do not preserve geometry. Inspect output.

Draw one path per intended structural line; never edge-detect a thick line into two parallel outlines. Remove construction frames, panel envelopes, wrist/cap baselines and tile borders while retaining real organic contours and intentional cuffs. Leave decorative seam paths open for continuation. Do not join endpoints along a template edge. Keep captions, IDs, crop marks, alignment and relief-cut guides on a separate placement sheet, outside transfer artwork.

Use crisp black lines on white, structural overlaps, identifying features, exact lettering and selected shading boundaries. Remove grey, solid shadow fills, texture, stippling and hatching. Do not trace every tonal band. Keep a separate shaded reference. Evaluate thickness and spacing at final physical size and on actual printer; do not promise a universal line minimum.

Keep all exports in one coordinate system. Overlay-check registration and inspect joins full size. Generated conversions can drift: label drafts until registration is verified. Re-shading a similar generated outline is not proof of an exact trace. Never claim perfect, exact or seamless merely from resemblance.

Set final dimensions, printable width, media, margins and DPI for actual printer. Tile at 100% with documented repeated artwork for registration; number tiles on assembly guide. Prevent double-transfer of overlap strokes. Keep a 100 mm calibration mark on separate test guide and measure the print. Confirm mirroring once using device process and a directional test or lettering. Never fit-to-page or double mirror.

For sleeves invoke tattoo-sleeve-layout, keeping measured landmarks and no-ink zones on placement layer. Complete paper/client fit before claiming fit verified. If dimensions are missing deliver useful drafts and specify unresolved fit without inventing measurements.

Read references/production-sources.md for research and limits.
