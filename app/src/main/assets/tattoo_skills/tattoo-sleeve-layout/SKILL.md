# Tattoo Sleeve Layout

Preserve approved motifs, complexity, focal subjects, exclusions and artist style. Simplify tiny congestion first. For ornamental blades retain nested hooks, negative pockets and depth; never replace an intricate reference with generic waves. Change only the requested layer. Do not add an arm silhouette to standalone art unless requested.

## Measure before production

Record left/right side, coverage, primary viewing face, seam, excluded axilla and existing tattoos. Measure upper arm below axilla, widest relaxed biceps, elbow in documented posture, widest forearm and wrist at finish. Record landmark lengths, units, posture and front/outside/back views; obtain shoulder tracing or marked photos. No universal adult male size guarantees fit.

Use width at each height as circumference; keep elbow at its measured position. Adapt sections independently instead of uniformly scaling the sleeve. A shoulder is compound curved: develop a cap with adequate height, fit with paper relief cuts and refine on client. Do not flatten the cap or compress upper artwork to fit a page. Keep axilla excluded; loosen elbow transitions and keep important detail away from high-distortion folds.

## Match joins

Use a canonical coordinate system across upper arm, elbow, forearm and cap. Pair seam endpoints by height, position, tangent direction, stroke width and nearby values. Inspect opposite edges side by side and rolled; redraw incompatibilities. Place application sections through forgiving negative space. A shoulder cap attaches to a measured arc, not necessarily the entire upper-arm circumference.

Keep separate layers for art contours, shading and placement/cut guides. Template sides, cap base, wrist baseline and tile edges are not tattoo contours. Let decorative paths continue openly at seams. Never connect endpoints with an enclosing outline, doubled border or line around a tapered panel. Preserve intentional cuffs and real design edges. Relief cuts and overlaps are paper operations, not ink.

## Verify outputs

Produce separate shaded art, transfer-only lines and placement guide from identical master geometry and dimensions. Put IDs, elbow marks, seam keys and cut/calibration guides outside transfer artwork. Invoke tattoo-stencil-production for printing. Use calibrated 100% scale after dimensions are set; tile with documented overlap. Verify mirroring for the specific device; never double mirror.

Check paper wrap and client placement with neutral posture and elbow movement. Record corrections and artist sign-off. Call unmeasured art a fit concept and unregistered generated outlines drafts. Do not call either exact, seamless, print-to-fit or tattoo-ready.

Read references/sources.md for research and references/layout-contract.md for portable records. Run scripts/check_layout.py to check recorded evidence completeness; this does not certify physical fit.
