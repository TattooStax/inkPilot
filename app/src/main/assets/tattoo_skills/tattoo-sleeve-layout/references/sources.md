# Research and scope
Reviewed 2026-11-14. Process references, not universal standards or evidence that a generated panel fits.
- https://terryribera.com/body-wrap-tattoo-template-app — artist-developed unrolled measured templates, circumference widths, fixed joint landmarks, seam choices and true-size print. App was still in testing when reviewed; body fitting is still needed.
- https://tattooing101.com/learn/tips-advice/how-to-apply-tattoo-stencils/ — prepositioning and alignment marks.
- https://tattooing101.com/learn/guides/how-to-make-a-tattoo-stencil/ — line-weight tracing and machine mirror controls; verify device instructions.
- https://www.stencilai.app/blog/how-to-make-a-large-tattoo-stencil — true-size tiling, repeat alignment strips, backside tape and section placement. Its sheet-size assumptions do not cover all roll printers.
- https://phomemo.com/blogs/news/how-to-print-a-large-tattoo-stencil-a-guide-for-sleeves-legs-back-pieces — vendor example of continuous printing; verify device media and printable width.
Keeping construction boundaries off transfer artwork and matching seam tangents are design/engineering requirements derived from artist feedback, not universal regulatory protocol from these sources.
