# Reusable lessons from reference work

Use source geometry, client meaning and the artist's own style profile, not a generic tattoo aesthetic.
- Portraits: preserve eye spacing, expression, muzzle/jaw and light facial markings. Avoid exaggerated dark cheeks or angry brows that change identity. Honor closed mouths and no-teeth constraints. Validate animal limb attachments and perspective.
- Smooth black-grey: broad brush/mag-shading-friendly gradients, selective black anchors, open whites; no tiny fur strokes, whiskers, microdots, scratches or hatch texture unless specifically requested. Adjust line weights selectively rather than boldening everything.
- Flowers: natural overlapping petals and readable centers, purposeful size hierarchy, forward-facing or tilted as requested. Keep leaves on coherent stems with restrained veins; do not fill every gap or add awkward floating foliage.
- Nature/backgrounds: simpler rock masses and clear water ribbons; preserve requested waterfall counts. Clouds/rays use negative space and soft edges when requested. No invented birds/objects.
- Lettering: exact spelling, correct handwriting, legible spacing, no mirror reversal. For technical motifs such as constellations verify topology/orientation from authoritative sources; decorative style does not authorize warping the pattern.
- Ornamental blades: retain major/medium nested hooks, direction, negative pockets and depth. Simplify tiny slivers first; avoid replacing detailed identity with generic waves. Maintain shoulder height and avoid compression.
- Portfolio edits: preserve the actual tattoo and honest result. Make only requested conservative photographic changes; do not redraw healed work, alter pigmentation or imply generated improvements were tattooed.
- Deliver requested versions as separate images, preserve approved elements and change one requested layer per iteration. Keep stencil, shaded reference, placement mockup and assembly guide separate.

These are design heuristics, not claims of universal line sizes, tattoo durability or verified client fit. Current explicit artist/client instructions supersede defaults.
