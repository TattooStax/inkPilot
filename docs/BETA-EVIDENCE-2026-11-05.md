## Ordered gates to a functional text beta

1. **Close code and hosted evidence.** Reconcile callback success with exact native SMS/MMS row updates and durable scheduled verdicts; retain `UNKNOWN` after any unverified part. Run provider failure and multipart transition tests. Make the corrected identity verifier, shell alias test and emulator carrier fixture green on one published source SHA. Keep the optional bridge disabled until its posture and provider-visible send contract is verified.
2. **Prove one owned interface.** Exercise cold/warm entry for inbox, new SMS, thread, attachment/media, held draft, notifications, shortcuts, supported shares, contacts, Settings, app lock and Back. Preserve unsent recipient/text/media through rotation, incoming refresh and restart; retain five tabs on each product screen. Measure accessibility on the phone.
3. **Qualify local drafts.** Ground responses in actual reviewed studio/contact/calendar context. Reject unsupported booking, price, medical, image and phantom-action claims before staff sees a draft. Run the full synthetic set, blind staff scoring, and at least 20 consecutive offline turns on the intended phone with latency, memory, battery and thermal measures. Every send and booking stays subject to staff approval.
4. **Run the exact carrier matrix.** Record the installed APK hash and role holders, rehearse rollback, then exercise incoming SMS → held draft → explicit approval → visible native row → recipient delivery. Add multipart/non-ASCII, MMS image, failed callback, airplane mode, process death, dual SIM, role removal and same/near-duplicate arrivals. Distinguish carrier acceptance from delivery at the other handset.
5. **Complete staff and recovery workflow.** Walk a lead through contact, memory correction, references/albums, assigned artist, appointment and reminder in the same app. Prove original images, human drafts, schedules and native messages survive upgrade, restart and supported export/import. Qualify a stable signed canonical installation and migration path before customer data moves.
6. **Qualify manual phone separately.** Test Phone-role change/rollback on a sacrificial device window, real incoming and outgoing calls, lock-screen notification controls, denied alert permissions, SIM and audio route handling. AI voice answering requires a proven audio path and a separate implementation and acceptance plan; it is not part of this text beta.

**Current verdict: not functional-beta qualified.** The original audit's roughly 40% estimate described its older source snapshot, not today's measured completion. Source and selected device tests have advanced, but a new percentage would obscure the open hosted, semantic-model and carrier gates. Reassess only after the same installed candidate passes the complete lead-to-appointment path and the blocking gates above. Do not treat the ordered list as a promise that implementation alone passes acceptance.
